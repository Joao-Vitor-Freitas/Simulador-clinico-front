## Tasks

1. Create an account and start a case.
2. Identify the patient's visible symptoms and at least one vital sign.
3. Ask the patient a question or describe a conduct in text.
4. Reveal a protocol for a selected symptom.
5. Report an incoherent answer if one is shown; otherwise explain where that control is.
6. Finish the case and find the diagnosis, elapsed time, and feedback.

Let each person attempt the tasks without guiding clicks. Record task completion, hesitation, wrong turns, comments, and whether help was needed. Do not collect real patient information or credentials in this document.
